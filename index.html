Everyone in the photo. Even you.

Allfie is an iPhone camera for group photos. Take the shot, swap places with a friend, take one more, and Allfie puts you into the picture. Took a few shots where someone blinked? Pick the best face for each person and get one photo where everyone looks good.

Add Me. Two shots from the same spot, merged into one, so the person holding the phone is finally in it.

Best Take. Pick each person's best face from a few shots: eyes open, smiling, looking at the camera.

Private by design. Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no tracking.
