Support

Need help with Allfie? Email imthiyasyusuf1@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who made it, usually within two working days.

How do I get myself into the group photo?

Open Add Me. Take the first photo of the group, leaving a gap for yourself. Hand the phone to someone in the photo, step into the gap, and they line up the see through outline of the first shot and take the second. Allfie merges the two.

Can I use photos I already took?

Yes. Choose Import and pick two photos taken from about the same spot.

How does Best Take work?

Take or import 2 to 10 shots of the same group. Allfie picks the best face for each person, and you can tap any face to choose a different one.

Tips for a clean merge

Stand in the same place for both shots, keep the background the same, and leave clear space between people.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Allfie Pro.

Is my data safe?

Allfie does not collect any data and your photos never leave your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
