Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Allfie iPhone app ("the app"), made by Yusuf Imthiyas, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom.

The short version

Allfie does not collect any data. Your photos never leave your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third party services in the app.

Photos and camera

The app asks for camera access so you can take photos, and photo library access so you can import photos and save your results. All processing, including finding people and faces and merging shots, happens on your device using Apple's built in frameworks. Photos are not uploaded, shared or sent to us or anyone else. Face information is used only in the moment to line up and blend your photos and is not stored, used to identify anyone, or used for any other purpose.

Data we collect

None. The app makes no network requests for analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not know who you are or what you photograph.

Purchases

If you buy Allfie Pro, the payment is handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small count of how many results you have saved (for the free limit) and your settings, on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
