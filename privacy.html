Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Allfie iPhone app ("the app"), made by Yusuf Imthiyas, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom.

The short version

Allfie collects nothing. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and never leave it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third party services in the app. It works fully offline.

Photos and camera

The app asks for camera access so you can take photos, and photo library access so you can import photos and save your results. All processing, including finding people and faces and merging shots, happens on your device using Apple's built in frameworks. Photos are not uploaded, shared or sent to us or anyone else. Face information is used only in the moment to line up and blend your photos. It is not stored, not used to identify anyone and not used for anything else.

Data we collect

None. We don't run servers for the app, and the app doesn't send anything to us. We don't know who you are or what you photograph.

Optional tips

Allfie is free with no limits and no subscription. If you choose to leave a tip, Apple handles the payment through your Apple ID using the App Store. We never see your name, card or payment details. Apple only tells us, in anonymous sales totals, that a tip was bought. Tips unlock nothing. The only time the app talks to the internet is when the tip screen asks the App Store for prices or you buy a tip.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and your saved results stay on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
